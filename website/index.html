The System

How Phony Baloney works

A fully automated voice loop — no human needed. Harold handles everything.

01
📱

Scammer Calls In

A known or suspected scam number dials in. Twilio receives the call and routes it to Harold.

02
👴

Harold Picks Up

The bot answers in Harold's AI-generated voice: "Oh hello? One second dear, let me turn down the television…"

03
👂

Listen & Classify

The scammer's speech is transcribed in real time. The system detects the scam type and loads Harold's matching playbook.

04
🤔

Harold Gets Confused

A language model generates Harold's response: hard of hearing, fumbling for a pen, going on a tangent about his cat. Every word is designed to buy more time.

05
🔊

Harold Talks Back

The response is synthesized into Harold's voice using AI text-to-speech and played back to the scammer.

06
🔄

Repeat Until They Give Up

The loop continues — recording, transcribing, generating, speaking — wasting the scammer's most valuable resource: time.

07
🚫

Reported to Spam Registry

Once the call ends, the scammer's phone number is automatically added to a national spam caller registry — helping protect others from the same number.

The Agent

Meet Harold

Harold is 77 years old. He lives alone, watches a lot of TV, and has a cat named Biscuit.

He's hard of hearing. He mixes up numbers. He frequently needs to find a pen. He once spent four minutes looking for his Medicare card while narrating every step.

He is also completely fictional — a persona carefully engineered to maximize the time a scammer stays on the line.

Harold never breaks character. Harold never hangs up first.

🦻 Hard of hearing 🤷 Easily confused 📄 Slow with paperwork 🐱 Has a cat named Biscuit 💻 Grandson set up the computer 📍 Can never remember his new address 🕶️ Always looking for his glasses

Coverage

Harold is ready for anything

The system automatically classifies every call and loads the right playbook for Harold.

🏥

Medicare Fraud

"Your benefits are about to expire."

Harold's move: Slowly reads a wrong Medicare ID, corrects it, then says he needs his glasses to read the card properly. Mentions his recent trip to the doctor for a concerning situation.

🏛️

IRS Impersonation

"You'll be arrested if you don't act now."

Harold's move: Panics just enough to seem compliant, but can't find a pencil. Asks them to repeat the case number letter by letter. His computer is also loading very slowly.

💻

Tech Support

"Your computer has a virus."

Harold's move: Describes his screen incorrectly, asks what a browser is, mentions he's running Windows 98. Recently installed YouTube for aerobics videos.

🏠

Mortgage Refinance

"We can lower your rate today."

Harold's move: Mentions his late wife used to handle these things. Attempts to find the account number in a filing cabinet. Narrates the entire search.

❤️

Romance Scam

"I've fallen for you…"

Harold's move: Asks them to spell their name again. Goes on a lengthy tangent about Biscuit the cat. Describes a TV show plot in full detail.

📞

Unknown / Generic

Anything else.

Harold's move: Turns down the TV, asks what company they're calling from, and wonders if they have family in Utah. He has a great restaurant story about that.

What's Next

The Roadmap

Harold is just getting started. Here's what's coming.

🔍
Classification

Smarter Spam Registry

Today, numbers get flagged automatically. Next up: post-call analysis that classifies calls before adding anything to the registry — fewer false positives, more confidence in every entry.

⚡
Performance

Realtime API

Swapping the STT → LLM → TTS pipeline for OpenAI's Realtime API. Faster turn-taking means Harold sounds more natural — and scammers stay on the line even longer.

🤖
Detection

Bot Detection & Early Cutoff

Not all scammers are human anymore. We're building detection for robocallers and AI dialers — so Harold can ID bot callers and hang up before wasting anyone's compute.
